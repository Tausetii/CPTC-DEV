WORMHOLE RESORT & CASINO
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (rev. 2025-01)

1. Welcome
Welcome to the Wormhole Resort & Casino family. This handbook outlines
expectations for all employees across our Mars Vegas HQ, Reno, and Atlantic
City branches.

2. Code of conduct
Treat every guest and colleague with respect. Report incidents to your
manager or to HR via the internal portal.

3. Time-off policy
Full-time employees accrue 15 PTO days per year, plus 10 paid holidays.
